# Zomato-Business-Analytics
Analyzed Zomato sales performance, order trends, customer ratings, and revenue metrics to identify growth opportunities for restaurant outlets.
Evaluated discount effectiveness, campaign performance, and customer acquisition trends to improve profitability and order volume.
Developed Excel/Power BI dashboards to track KPIs such as Orders, Average Order Value (AOV), Revenue, Conversion Rate, and Customer Retention.
Performed location-wise and category-wise analysis to recommend pricing, promotional, and marketing strategies that increased online sales visibility.
Conducted trend and variance analysis on sales, discounts, and customer ratings to support business decision-making.
